Explore the landscape

Start with the curated landscape, or explore every company and product profile. Choose a layer, search a company, founder or investor, then open a profile to see its research and sources.

A map of roles, not rankings

Position reflects each profile’s role in the ecosystem. Companies can span several layers. Every logo has the same display size; size does not represent funding, maturity or importance.

Find, save, compare

Switch between Map and List without losing your filters. Click a founder, investor or category in a profile to explore related companies. Save profiles to compare up to four side by side. Saved profiles stay in this browser.

Read the evidence

Each profile includes its research status, last reviewed date and available source links. “Verified” is a research status, not an endorsement or an audit of company performance. Funding descriptions may cover different dates and financing types; compare the original descriptions rather than treating them as a ranking.

Geography

Geography usually uses headquarters or a reported company base. Explicit editorial ecosystem classifications, such as an Israeli R&D base, are labelled in the profile and distinguished from headquarters. Each profile shows its basis and source. Profiles with two documented company bases can appear in both region filters. Europe includes the UK and Switzerland. Other includes known locations outside the four named regions; Unknown means a reliable location is still missing. Headquarters is separate from founder nationality, company origins and overseas offices.

Keyboard navigation

Use Tab to move between controls, Enter to open a profile, and Escape to close it. Press / to focus search. On smaller screens, details open in a focused dialog.
